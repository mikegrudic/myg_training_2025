For whole-mile distance budgets up to 31 miles and the exact race distances (5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon, 50K), standing in for the nearest whole mile, the route that climbs the most in five closed shapes and one-way to another trailhead. Each map shows the route over the surrounding trail network, with the start as a circle and a one-way finish as a square. Select a route for its profile and turn-by-turn trail list.

Gain is from USGS 3DEP elevation smoothed over 50 m, so a watch will usually read somewhat higher (the unsmoothed figure is in each route's details). Every loop is at least 1 mile and at least a quarter of the route's distance; only out-and-back trail, stems and bridges are run twice. Roads can link trails, but road time (by the Strava grade-adjusted-pace model) is capped at 10% of the route's.

Gold, silver and bronze outlines mark the three shapes with the most gain at each distance; ties share a medal. Shaded rows are race distances. A one-way route ends where a trail meets a road open to cars that OpenStreetMap doesn't mark as unpaved; most roads carry no surface tag and count as paved. Arrange the return before you go. Trails closed on the NY-NJ Trail Conference's December 2025 Bear Mountain area status map are excluded; check current conditions before you go.
